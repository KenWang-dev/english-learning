## Iteration 1 — L1 Daily Basics (2026-03-13)

### Process Breakdown

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Step 1 | Meeting Up（碰面汇合）|
| Step 2 | Getting Tickets（取票/买票）|
| Step 3 | Grabbing Snacks（买零食）|
| Step 4 | Finding Seats（找座位入场）|

### Dialogue

You: Hey babe, I just got off work. I'm at the mall. Where are you?
Her: I'm almost there! Give me five minutes.

*(You meet at the entrance)*

You: Got it. I already bought the tickets online. Let me pull up the QR code.
Her: Nice. What time does it start?
You: 7:30. We still have time. Wanna grab some popcorn?

Her: Yes! A large popcorn and two drinks?
You: Sure. Excuse me, can we get a large popcorn and two iced lemon teas, please?
Staff: That'll be sixty-eight. Anything else?
You: That's it, thanks.

*(You walk into the theater)*

You: We're in Row 8, seats 5 and 6. Over there.
Her: Oh nice, these are great seats.

*(After the movie)*

Her: That was so good! The ending totally caught me off guard.
You: Right? I did NOT see that coming. Wanna grab dinner? I'm starving.
Her: Absolutely. Let's go.

*(~140 words)*

### Learning Notes

**Vocabulary & Phrases**
- Give me five minutes — "给我五分钟"，比 "wait for me" 更自然的口语
- pull up — 调出（手机上的东西），如 pull up the QR code（调出二维码）
- caught me off guard — 出乎意料，比 "surprised me" 更生动
- I did NOT see that coming — "完全没想到"，口语中 NOT 重读表示强调

**Core Patterns**
- "I already bought the tickets online." — 提前买好票的标准说法
- "Can we get a large popcorn and two [drinks], please?" — 柜台点单万能句，get 比 have 更北美口语
- "We're in Row 8, seats 5 and 6." — 找座位的标准表达，Row + 数字, seats + 数字

**Synonym Swap**
- TOP1: "That was so good!" ← 最常用的观后好评
- TOP2: "That was amazing!" — 语气更强
- TOP3: "I loved it!" — 更个人化的表达

**Cultural Tip**
- 北美/国际影院用 Row + Seat 编号找座位，口语中直接说数字即可，不用加 "number"
- "babe" 是情侣间常见称呼，正式程度类似中文"宝贝"，日常非常高频
